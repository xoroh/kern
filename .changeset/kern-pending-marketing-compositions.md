Add reusable web marketing compositions to `@xoroh/kern/start`.

- **`Hero`**: responsive page opening with heading, copy, action, and art slots.
- **`TopBar`**: responsive site navigation with host-owned route state, an action slot, and an accessible mobile disclosure.
- **`SiteFooter`**: data-driven footer with router-compatible internal links, external links, and legal/trailing slots.
